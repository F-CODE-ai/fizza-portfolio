# Projects

## Sentinel AI - Live Scam Call Detection
GIKI Advanced AI Bootcamp 2026 capstone, team of 3.

- Problem: scam phone calls trick people in real time, before they realise what is happening.
- What we built: a real-time scam-call detection system using a multi-agent LangGraph pipeline with 6 specialised workers: pattern detection, semantic matching, emotional-manipulation analysis, social-engineering analysis, organisation verification and compliance scoring. Backend: FastAPI with WebSockets. Frontend: React + Electron.
- My role: I owned the frontend, the UI and testing, including fixing a live microphone input bug.
- Result: presented to the GIKI Rector as a winning-project showcase.
- Code: https://github.com/Hassan-Raza-Shaikh/Live-Scam-Calling-Detection

## AttendX - Face-Recognition Attendance System
Academic project, team of 2.

- Problem: manual classroom attendance is slow and easy to fake.
- What we built: an attendance system using ArcFace (ResNet-50, 512-dimensional embeddings) for face recognition, with eye-aspect-ratio blink liveness detection, wave-gesture check-out and multi-face spoofing rejection. Designed for a classroom of about 45 students.
- Tools: InsightFace/ArcFace, MediaPipe, FastAPI, Docker, SQLite, Gradio UI.
- Code: https://github.com/muhammadumerfarooqq/attendance-system

## TALEEM360 - AR/VR STEM Education App (Final Year Project)
- Problem: school students find abstract STEM topics such as human anatomy hard to picture from flat textbook images.
- What we are building: an AR application that delivers interactive 3D anatomical models (for example the human heart) for the school-level STEM curriculum.
- Status: in progress. We are revising the demo and the app architecture after instructor feedback on the first submission.
